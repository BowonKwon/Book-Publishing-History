Method

Three layers of numbers

The numbers on this site fall into three layers.

Layer What it is Examples
Compiled Estimates published by other researchers, redrawn as given Buringh and van Zanden manuscript and printed-book estimates; Fink-Jensen titles per capita
Official tables Values transcribed from government or trade statistics Korean Publishers Association, Statistics Bureau of Japan, National Bureau of Statistics of China, US Census Bureau, WIPO
Counted here Open bibliographic data recounted for this site VD16–18, STCN, ISTC, KORCIS

Rules for the counts made here

The rules were written down before counting, and the time was recorded in the version history (git commits). Changes made after seeing results are listed in the next section.

VD16, VD17, VD18. From the merged metadata released by the Berlin State Library in 2025 (CC0, 750,342 rows), only records of physical items (bibliographic-type field beginning with A) that are monographs (a) or multivolume works as a whole (c) were counted. Single volumes (f), periodicals (b, v), and contained works (s) were left out. A VD16, VD17 or VD18 number was required. The year is the leading four-digit number of the date field (the normalized year). Records outside 1450–1830 or with no four-digit year were counted separately in the audit file (944 without a year, 6 out of range). The language is the first code in the language field; codes marking the original language of a translation ($c) are not treated as text languages.

STCN. From the linked data of the National Library of the Netherlands, the 217,761 entities of type "Book" in the STCN dataset were counted. The year is the first four digits of the start date of the publication event. The 19,389 records dated only to a decade or century (such as "17XX") were left out as undated. Language values have no order, so a single value is taken as the language and two or more are grouped as "multiple." The endpoint does not accept sorted queries beyond 10,000 rows, so the aggregation was done on the server.

ISTC. From the British Library's 2025 release (CC BY-NC 4.0), 30,587 records, the single date was used where given and otherwise the start of the date range (5,155 records). The 87 records without a date were left out. The 1,970 records dated after 1500 are counted separately; the charts show 1452–1500. Records are not republished; only counts are.

KORCIS. The open API of the Korean Old and Rare Collection Information System (National Library of Korea) can only be queried by search term. All results for twenty terms fixed in advance (the digits 0–9 and 卷, 朝鮮, 未詳, 集, 書, 記, 錄, 文, 經, 傳) were collected and deduplicated by record number. Searching by digits alone would favor records that print a year, so 未詳 ("unknown") and 朝鮮 (which also catches period labels such as "late Joseon") were included. These are surviving holding records, not counts of publications; one book held in several libraries appears several times.

Changed after seeing results

In the VD place-of-publication count, the first results showed missing places counted as a city named "nan," and the same city split between "Leipzig" and the authority-linked form "!104798998!Leipzig ; ID: gnd/4035206-7." Missing values were moved to "no place," and names were merged after removing the authority prefix and punctuation. Year and language counts were not affected.

In the KORCIS year reading, a sample showed that some date fields use CJK compatibility ideographs (14,976 fields; most often U+F967 for 不), so "unknown" markers were missed. Date fields are now normalized (NFKC) before reading.

Comparing with an independent recount made from the raw files and the rules above found four places where the code departed from the rules, now fixed. The ISTC count applied an undocumented year window (1440–1520) that sent 84 dated records to "undated" and missed one date written with a leading blank (" 1485"). In KORCIS, 明治, 大正 and 昭和 were missing from the era list, so 55 records fell into "other." The exact grouping key for KORCIS and the handling of quotation marks in VD place lists were also corrected. The values on this site are those after the fixes.

Discrepancies found between sources

  • China, new titles in 1990: 55,254 in the 1999 yearbook, 55,245 in the 2024 yearbook.
  • Gosa chwaryo woodblock list: provincial counts sum to 987; the stated total is 989 (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture).
  • Our World in Data manuscript table: France alone is stored at years 50 past the start of each century (550, 650, …).
  • Our World in Data printed-book table: the notes give the unit as "title or edition," but the values are about 441 times the number of ISTC editions for the same period.
  • Korean Publishers Association series: the number of publishers goes from 1,962 in 1969 to 1,016 in 1970, and copies from 157,510,364 in 1998 to 75,840,951 in 1999, with no change of definition noted in the footnotes for either.

What was not done

  • Separate sources were not spliced into one line.
  • No new per capita values were computed (only the Fink-Jensen table was redrawn).
  • Rankings of authors or publishers, bestseller lists, and the share of women authors are not covered.

A History of Book Publishing

From manuscripts to e-books: how many books appeared each year, and how they were counted

How many books appeared in a given year? The numbers that answer this question change character from one era to the next. For the Middle Ages they are estimates worked back from surviving manuscripts; for early modern Europe they are union catalogs that list, edition by edition, the books that libraries still hold; from the nineteenth century on they are statistics produced by the book trade and by governments. This site puts these numbers side by side, one line per source, and marks the years in which a source says it changed the way it counts.

The open union catalogs (VD16 to VD18 for the German-speaking lands, the STCN for the Netherlands, and the ISTC for incunabula) were recounted for this site. Official tables for Korea, Japan, China and the United States were transcribed from the originals and checked against them. The site does not rank authors or publishers.

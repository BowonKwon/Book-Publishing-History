Chapter 6. After digital (2000–)

Key point. After 2000 there are more numbers, but within one country and one year they can differ several times over depending on what is counted. In 2022 Japan registered 902,311 ISBNs; new titles shipped through distributors that year numbered 66,885.

One country, different numbers

Each format gets its own ISBN. WIPO's The Global Publishing Industry in 2022 notes that with ISBN data "there will inevitably be some double counting." ISBN registrations in 2022 were 338,237 in Korea and 902,311 in Japan. In the same year, new titles deposited with the Korean Publishers Association numbered 61,181, and the Shuppan Shihyo Nenpo counted 66,885 new titles in Japan. Japan's registrations rose by 717,326 from 184,985 in 2021; the report does not explain why.

Korea has yet another "titles" figure. The publishing distribution network of the Korea Publication Industry Promotion Agency reports 644,013 titles for 2024, but that is the number of different titles sold in bookstores that year. It measures something other than new publications and is not charted here.

Japan: the overlap of two series

The Japan Statistical Yearbook cited the Shuppan Nenkan through its 68th edition and the Shuppan Shihyo Nenpo from the 69th. The two overlap in 2015 and 2016:

Year Shuppan Nenkan Shuppan Shihyo Nenpo
2015 80,048 76,445
2016 78,113 75,039

In the Shuppan Shihyo Nenpo, new titles are those "estimated to be shipped on consignment or purchase terms as a new publication," excluding reprints (Japan Statistical Yearbook note). The Shuppan Nenkan ended with its 2018 edition. This is why the Japan line splits in two after 2005 in the Chapter 5 chart.

China: new titles and reprints

The NBS tables give all titles and, separately, new titles. New titles are 58.0 percent of the total in 1952, 58.8 percent in 2000, 57.6 percent in 2010, and 40.2 percent in 2023, when the total is 540,274. The 2024 bulletin of the National Press and Publication Administration reports 227,800 new and 322,000 reprinted titles and notes that the figures exclude Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan.

New titles for 1990 appear as 55,254 in the 1999 yearbook and 55,245 in the 2024 yearbook: two editions from the same agency differ by nine in one cell.

Korea: the recent KPA series

New titles deposited with the association number 43,598 in 2005, 45,213 in 2015, 65,792 in 2020, and 64,991 in 2025. Translations are 14.9 percent of the 2025 total. In 2025, 5,766 publishers deposited books with the association, and 85,689 publishers were registered in the publisher and printer search system (KPA 2025 statistics, p. 1).
